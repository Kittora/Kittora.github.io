LiquidCat

Available · v0.4.6

Download, convert and re-encode video and audio in one native app, powered by yt-dlp and FFmpeg.

  • GPU encoding with NVENC, AMF, Quick Sync, VAAPI and VideoToolbox
  • Batch import, queue, history and presets
  • 15 themes and 6 languages, on Windows, Linux and macOS

Meowgnify

In development

Upscale video and images with AI and GPU shaders, and compare the result side by side.

  • Real-ESRGAN, Real-CUGAN, Waifu2x, Anime4K and Upscayl

Our products

Two apps, one philosophy: powerful inside, simple outside.

LiquidCat

Download, convert and re-encode video & audio in one native Rust app. Powered by yt-dlp and FFmpeg, with GPU acceleration, a queue, 14 themes and 6 languages. Windows, Linux and macOS.

Meowgnify

Upscale videos and images with AI and GPU shaders. Real-ESRGAN, Real-CUGAN, Waifu2x, Anime4K and Upscayl with before/after comparison.

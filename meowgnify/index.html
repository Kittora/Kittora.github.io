Meowgnify

Upscale video and images with AI.

Drop a file, pick the scale and let the AI do the rest. Compare the result with a before/after slider or lens.

Follow progress on GitHub Try LiquidCat meanwhile
What it will do

Sharper pixels, zero setup

Video

Real-ESRGAN, Real-CUGAN and Waifu2x for general content, frame by frame.

Anime

Anime4K and anime-tuned models, with a live before/after preview.

Images

Upscayl and Clarity to upscale photos and digital art up to 16×.

Comparison

Slider or lens view with adjustable zoom to compare the original with the result.

GPU by default

Neural networks and shaders on Vulkan (ncnn-vulkan and libplacebo).

All in one

Engines download on demand; nothing else to install.

LiquidCat

Download, convert and re-encode media in one native app.

From a link to a finished MP4 or MP3: LiquidCat handles downloading, converting and re-encoding in one place, powered by yt-dlp and FFmpeg.

Download LiquidCat View source

Version 0.4.6 · GPL-3.0 · All platforms

Features

Everything you need to grab & convert media

Download anything

Video or audio from YouTube and hundreds of other sites. Paste many links at once or load them from a .txt file.

Convert & trim

MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, AAC, Opus or Ogg. Re-encode local files and cut an exact segment.

GPU accelerated

NVENC, AMF, Intel Quick Sync, VAAPI or VideoToolbox, detected automatically, with a CPU fallback.

Queue, history & presets

Line up jobs, follow them in the floating Activity card and start from eight built-in presets.

15 themes, 6 languages

Catppuccin, Material 3, Fluent, Tokyo Night, Nord and more, each with its own cat. Follows your system accent color.

Native & self-updating

Rust + Slint with GPU rendering, around 60 MB at idle. yt-dlp and FFmpeg are installed and kept up to date for you.

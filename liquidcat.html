Everything you need to grab & convert media

From a YouTube link to a finished MP4 or MP3 — LiquidCat handles downloading, converting and re-encoding in one place.

Download anything

Grab video or audio from YouTube and hundreds of sites. Paste many links at once or load them from a .txt.

Convert & re-encode

Turn video into MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, AAC, Opus or Ogg, re-encode local files and trim a segment.

GPU accelerated

Hardware encoding with NVENC, AMF, Intel Quick Sync, VAAPI or VideoToolbox, detected automatically.

Queue, history & presets

Line up jobs, follow them in the floating Activity card and start from eight built-in presets.

14 themes, 6 languages

Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Nord, Gruvbox, Neo Brutalist & more, each with its own cat. EN, ES, PT, FR, DE, IT.

Native & lightweight

Rewritten in Rust with Slint and GPU rendering. ~60 MB at idle, no WebView, and yt-dlp / FFmpeg are kept up to date for you.
